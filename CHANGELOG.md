## 1.0.0

- The API is stable; `Lame.encode` is unchanged from 0.1.0.
- The built libraries are now tested on an Android emulator and an iOS
  simulator. The ABI boundary test also checks a digest of the encoder
  output.

## 0.1.0

- First release: `Lame.encode` with LAME 3.100 on Android, iOS, macOS,
  Linux, Windows and the web.
